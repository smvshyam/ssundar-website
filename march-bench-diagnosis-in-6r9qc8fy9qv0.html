How the four weeks run
Week 1 · Access and shadow
Data access agreed, legal scope signed, time on the floor with the account.
Week 2 · Connect the data
Dialer statistics, quality scores, attendance and assessments mapped to the ramp. Analysed on Treeng, SSUNDAR’s own sixteen-module build.
Week 3 · Quantify
The forecast, the tenure view and the baseline, built and checked with your controller.
Week 4 · Decide
Findings presented to the leaders who own delivery, training and workforce planning. Go or no-go.
