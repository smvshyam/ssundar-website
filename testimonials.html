CRITICISM
04 — The criticism

What participants asked us to change.

Every improvement request in the dataset is published below, unedited. They are reproduced in full because of what they have in common.

01

“I would recommend at least 3 weeks for this program to be optimal.”

02

“Could have been 3-4 week learning […] felt the concepts were taken through a lot faster.”

03

“The improvement would be to extend these sessions. We have worked on a lot of case studies and working on more case studies would have been amazing.”

04

“While I understand the need to compress the program into a single day, I believe its impact could have been even greater had it been spread over 2–3 days.”

05

“I found every part of the program valuable, and would especially welcome more sessions led by Shyam.”

06

“Shyam can facilitate some TL's, Managers and Leads already working on the floor as well.”

Every request was for more time. None were for different content.

Method

Source: participant survey data, 27 responses, 22 with usable free text, across two 2025 cohorts — a leadership cohort (Aug–Sep 2025, n=11) and a capability co-design workshop (Jul 2025, n=16). Client organization names, programme names and all colleague and facilitator names have been removed under NDA and will not be restored. No role titles exist in the source data and none have been inferred; sector, seniority band and year is the maximum claim made for any quote. Every quote is reproduced verbatim — cuts marked “[…]”, clarifying insertions in square brackets. Nothing has been rewritten or corrected.
