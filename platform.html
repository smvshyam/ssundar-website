Five Judgment Domains

The diagnostic assesses judgment infrastructure across five domains. Each scores independently and contributes to an overall Judgment Infrastructure Rating.

01

Decision Quality Architecture

How decisions are structured, who holds them, and whether quality is defined and measured.

02

Accountability Infrastructure

Whether accountability is real and consequential, or nominal and diffused.

03

AI-Integration Readiness

The judgment maturity of teams and structures that will be most exposed to AI augmentation.

04

Talent Judgment Signals

Whether the organisation selects, develops, and promotes for judgment quality — or proxies.

05

Systemic Failure Patterns

The recurring decision failure modes that persist across leadership layers and business cycles.

Global Organisational Judgment Index 2026

The GOJI is a first-of-kind global benchmark of organisational judgment infrastructure.

Every diagnostic completed on the SSUNDAR Platform contributes anonymised data to the GOJI — a live index tracking the judgment infrastructure of organisations across sectors, geographies, and transformation maturity levels.

The 2026 edition will be the first comprehensive picture of whether the world's organisations have the judgment architecture to support the AI investments they are making. Publication is planned for Q4 2026.

Contributing organisations receive advanced access to the sector-specific benchmarks relevant to their industry.
