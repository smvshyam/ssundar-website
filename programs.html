Quality and Performance Turnaround

60 to 90 days · Contact centres and operations below target

The problem

An account has been below target for months. More training has not moved it, and the client has started counting.

What we build

Judgment standards rebuilt at the point of customer contact, then held across the floor. The systems are fixed, not the people blamed.

What changes

  • Quality recovers inside one quarter and holds after the team leaves
  • Team leaders know which calls to coach, and why
  • A written baseline the client can see moving

How it is measured

Quality score, CSAT and escalation rate against the agreed baseline.

On record: 77 → 93% quality in 90 days on an escalated technical-support account while headcount ramped 70%. CSAT 70 → 89% across a 1,600-person BPO operation.

Onboarding Excellence System

4 to 6 months of system design, then ongoing support · L&D, operations and HR heads running large hiring

The problem

New hires take months to carry the work. Training feedback is high, floor performance is low, and early attrition peaks while people are still learning.

What we build

The onboarding architecture from offer to production-ready: diagnostic, curriculum and practice design, trainer certification, a controlled pilot, then scale.

What changes

  • A shorter path from offer to proficient seat
  • On-the-job training that refines instead of remediating
  • Fewer new hires lost before they are dependable
  • Training capacity that stops being the bottleneck

How it is measured

Days to production-ready performance, new-hire quality, 0 to 90 day attrition, and trainer throughput.

On record: 30-day new-hire CSAT 79 → 89%, with a second cohort 88 → 98%. About 30% fewer new-hire attritions and underperformers in a sales-outsourcing operation.

Performance Consulting

4 to 12 weeks · Operations where investment is not moving the numbers

The problem

The numbers are not moving despite the spend, and nobody can say which blocker is the real one.

What we build

Root-cause performance analysis, the systemic blockers ranked, and an improvement roadmap with measurable milestones.

What changes

  • A roadmap with owners, dates and milestones

How it is measured

The milestones agreed at the start, read against your own data.

On record: Resolution time 30 days → 4 in a compliance operation at a global e-commerce marketplace, errors down over 90%. Training was the last of five fixes, not the first.

Leadership Performance Architecture

Flagship · three-phase system · GCCs and technology organisations building leaders at scale

The problem

Leaders know the rules and follow the process, but decisions slow down or escalate the moment the situation is ambiguous.

What we build

A leadership system in three phases: compliance, which removes procedural risk; capability, which builds decision quality under ambiguity; and performance, which validates it under real pressure.

What changes

  • Faster decisions without more escalation
  • Leadership pipelines that scale
  • Behaviour change you can observe, not just report

How it is measured

Decision latency, escalation volume and observed behaviour against a baseline.

Leadership Readiness System

6 months · 2 days a week on site · 12 to 16 participants · High-potential managers moving into leadership

The problem

People are promoted for being excellent at the job they are leaving. Then they escalate decisions within their own authority, manage tasks instead of developing people, and stall.

What we build

Four phases: a diagnostic baseline, decision architecture, development architecture (coaching and feedback) and strategic integration, run alongside a consulting track with your leadership team.

What changes

  • Decisions made at the right level
  • Managers who develop their teams
  • A stronger succession bench
  • Shorter performance dips when people step up

How it is measured

Decision confidence, escalation volume, coaching conversations per direct report, and succession readiness.

Facilitator Certification (Train-the-Trainer)

5 days intensive + 30-day follow-up · 12 to 18 participants · Corporate trainers and L&D professionals

The problem

Training with great feedback that does not change behaviour, product experts who struggle to engage, and inconsistent quality across trainers.

What we build

Trainers who facilitate rather than present: learning science, design that transfers, facilitation technique, coaching and feedback, then certification through observed practice.

What changes

  • Learning that transfers to production
  • Consistent quality across the trainer team
  • Trainers who can coach, not only deliver
  • Less dependency on external facilitators

How it is measured

Observed facilitation against the certification standard, and transfer to the floor.

On record: 5.00 facilitator rating and 100% would recommend, across three live cohorts in Europe and Asia, scored by participants.
