THE SIMULATION

You will face 5 escalating organizational crises. Each is unique to your industry and scale — generated in real-time by AI. No two simulations are alike. Your decisions compound. Early choices reshape what's possible later.

THE TIMER — 45 SECONDS PER DECISION

Each round has a 45-second decision window. This measures decision velocity under pressure — a core leadership dimension. If time expires, the round freezes until you decide. There is no auto-selection. You always choose.

ALTITUDE = ORGANIZATIONAL HEALTH

You start at 10,000 feet (turbulence zone). Each decision shifts your altitude. Above 20,000 = strategic altitude. Above 28,000 = command altitude. Below 5,000 = danger zone. Your final altitude determines the report's assessment.

FIVE DIMENSIONS MEASURED

Every choice impacts: Continuity (institutional stability), Readiness (adaptability), Velocity (execution speed), Architecture (structural soundness), and Coherence (organizational alignment). Your pattern across these dimensions reveals your leadership archetype.

YOUR OWN APPROACH

Don't see an option you'd choose? Write your own strategy. AI analyzes your thinking in real-time and generates a custom consequence. This is where the simulation reveals the most about your judgment.
