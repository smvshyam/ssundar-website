60-Day Programme Structure

01

Weeks 1–2 · Foundation

Judgment Architecture Foundations

Participants develop a deep working understanding of judgment architecture theory — what it is, why it matters, and how it differs from competency-based and skills-based approaches. Includes the Judgment-Centered Leadership Design™ framework, the five domains of judgment infrastructure, and the theoretical underpinning of Play Labs™ methodology.

02

Weeks 3–5 · Facilitation Design

Scenario Design and Debrief Architecture

The most technically demanding phase. Participants learn to design Play Labs™ scenarios for their organisation's specific context, calibrate difficulty and ambiguity levels, and master the debrief framework that transforms observable decisions into judgment infrastructure data. Each participant designs, facilitates, and receives feedback on one original scenario.

03

Weeks 6–7 · AI Integration

Facilitating Judgment in AI-Augmented Contexts

How to facilitate judgment development specifically for teams where AI is part of the decision process. Includes AI over-reliance pattern recognition, deskilling facilitation, accountability architecture design when AI is in the loop, and how to calibrate the simulation environment to reflect AI-augmented conditions participants will actually face.

04

Weeks 8–9 · Certification

Live Delivery and Certification Assessment

Each participant delivers a full Play Labs™ session to a live cohort, assessed against the SSUNDAR Facilitator Certification Rubric. Written case reflection submitted alongside the delivery. Successful certification awards Play Labs™ Certified Facilitator status, which activates licensing rights under the applicable model.

What certification unlocks

Play Labs™ Certified Facilitator

Licensed to facilitate Play Labs™ sessions within the terms of the applicable licensing agreement.

Scenario Library Access

Access to the full SSUNDAR scenario library across 10 industries and 5 judgment domains.

SHRM-Track Pathway

Certification is on the SHRM co-accreditation pathway. Credits applicable to SHRM recertification requirements.

Annual Alumni Access

Certified facilitators join the SSUNDAR Academy alumni network with access to annual methodology updates and peer cohort sessions.

Co-Delivery Eligibility

Certified facilitators within qualifying organisations become eligible for the Play Labs™ Co-Delivery Partnership model.

Methodology Ownership

The deepest shift: facilitators finish the programme owning the methodology at an architectural level, not just the content level.
