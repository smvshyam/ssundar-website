The discipline

Diagnosis before prescription, always.

Every engagement at this tier starts the same way: a diagnosis that classifies the root cause as skills, structure, measurement or context. Three of those four are not training problems, and if the diagnosis lands there I will say so and the engagement ends.

That is not restraint for its own sake. A capability function that runs a program against a structural problem spends its credibility and solves nothing — and it is usually the second attempt, not the first, that costs the function its mandate.

Where the diagnosis does land on capability, the output of tier one is the brief for tier two: the architecture that makes the capability structural rather than episodic.
