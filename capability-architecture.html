The licensing principle

Licensed, never handed over.

Program IP is licensed per cohort or annually, with facilitator certification billed separately. Editable source is never handed over for a one-time fee.

That is not a commercial trick. A program whose source has been handed over drifts within two cohorts — the anchors soften, the hard scenarios get cut, and the measurement quietly disappears because it is the most uncomfortable part to run. Licensing keeps the designer in the room every time a cohort runs, which is the only reliable way the architecture survives contact with a calendar.

Where an organisation genuinely wants to own and extend the asset, that is Work Intelligence — where the ontology, the labelling protocol and the evaluation harness are designed to be handed over in full.
