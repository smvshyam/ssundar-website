Judgment-centered leadership design.

I build judgment into organisations. Not training programs. Four tiers, scoped by how deep the problem actually goes — from deciding what capability is for, through the architecture that makes it structural, to the intelligence layer that keeps it alive and the diagnostics that make it self-serve.

Most organisations arrive asking for a program. Where the diagnosis says the problem is structural — measurement, context or decision rights — a program spends credibility and solves nothing. Diagnosis comes first, always.

Tier 03 · the new service line

Work Intelligence: from programs to a capability operating system.

Every large operation already generates the signal it needs to see capability continuously — in its dialers, CRMs, quality tools, learning platforms and HR systems. Almost none connect that signal at the level of the person. Capability stays managed in arrears and by bucket.

The loop sense · connect · predict · act

SENSECapability signals from quality, transcripts, system behaviour, conversion, tenure, learning and HR events — each read where it already sits
CONNECTOne living capability record per associate, leader and trainer, mapped to the client’s own competency and indicator model
PREDICTReadiness, risk and next-best intervention per person per week — explainable to the indicator and the timestamp
ACTProactive nudges inside the tools people already use. Every correction becomes new signal

Human-in-the-loop by design: the system recommends, leaders decide. Outputs are a capability trajectory per cohort, a weekly readiness view for every leader, and one number finance can read — attrition and ramp cost avoided.

Tier 04 — Treeng productised diagnostics

Sixteen diagnostic engines for L&D, HR, operations, strategy, analytics, procurement and compliance. Named methodologies — Kirkpatrick-Phillips, ADDIE, Lean, Ishikawa — not a proprietary black box. Every finding carries a confidence grade of A, B or C based on the strength of the data behind it, and the engine names the data that would move a C up a grade. Output is an Excel workbook with live formulas and a deck you own outright. Free to start.
