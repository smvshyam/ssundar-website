The Methodology

Judgment is not a concept. It is an observable behaviour that emerges under conditions of pressure, ambiguity, and consequence.

Play Labs™ creates those conditions in a controlled facilitated environment. Participants are placed inside realistic organisational scenarios — with competing priorities, incomplete information, and a clock running — and asked to decide.

The facilitation process is designed to surface the judgment architecture at work: what frameworks are applied, what is weighted, what is ignored, and what the decision reveals about the organisation's deeper operating assumptions. This is the data. The debrief builds from there.

CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLES

01

Scenarios are built from real organisational failure patterns — not hypotheticals designed to teach.

02

No "right answer" is embedded. The value is in the decision process, not the outcome.

03

Debrief methodology is as rigorous as the scenario design. The debrief is where the architecture is built.

04

Designed for international application. Cultural calibration is built into the facilitation guide.
