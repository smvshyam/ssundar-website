How the work is run

Three habits that decide whether it holds.

Declare early
The plan goes to the client before they ask for it, and bad news arrives with a fix and a date, before the client finds it.
One owner, one date, one number
Every red flag leaves with a named action. The system recommends; a person decides.
Stop at the gates
Go or no-go at week 4 and week 16. If there is no material gap, the work says so and stops. Nothing is held back as leverage.
