01 · The leak

“It takes four months before a new hire is any use.”

You hire 100 for a requirement of 80. The loss happens before anyone is measuring.

The shortfall surfaces in a QBR as a line about refreshers and backfill, after the client has felt it. By then the penalty is booked and the team leaders have spent a quarter firefighting. The name for it is time to proficiency, and it is forecastable.

NeverSome never reach the floor. Offer-to-floor loss is absorbed as a hiring number, not a capability number.
LateSome reach it late. The seat is paid for weeks before it earns; graduation and proficiency are not the same day.
GoneSome leave before they are dependable. New-hire attrition is reported with tenured attrition, so its cause stays hidden.
