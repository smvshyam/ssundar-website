The problem is forecastable. Nobody forecasts it.

You hire 100 for a requirement of 80.

Some never reach the floor, some reach it late, some leave before they are dependable. The shortfall surfaces in a QBR as a line about refreshers and backfill, after the client has felt it. By then the penalty is booked and the team leaders have spent a quarter firefighting.

Never
Offer-to-floor loss is absorbed as a hiring number, not a capability number.
Late
Time to proficiency stretches, and the seat is paid for weeks before it earns.
Gone
New-hire attrition is reported with tenured attrition, so its real cause stays hidden.
Where it shows up

Five places an operation leaks capability. Most are managed as five separate problems.

Onboarding to on-floor speed
The weeks from offer to a proficient seat are rarely forecast per cohort. They decide whether the account makes margin this quarter.
Coaching that does not move the needle
Team leaders coach, but there is no cadence, no step-up target and no measure of whether behaviour changed.
Attrition that is not read by tenure
New-hire and tenured attrition have different causes and different fixes. Reported as one number, the real leak stays invisible until it is a staffing crisis.
Judgment that stalls at the first line
Managers can quote the framework and still freeze on the real call. Decisions cascade upward and escalations pile on senior leaders.
No forward view
Few operations can show a client, in writing, where its bench will stand in 90 days. The client feels the gap before you see it.

They are one problem: capability is never designed, measured or forecast as a system, so it surfaces late and expensively.

The offer

Three loops that feed each other.

A forward view, per account and per cohort, of whether you will have the people to deliver 30, 60 and 90 days from now, and the actions that keep that view true. Cheap signals already in your stack: dialer statistics, quality scores, attendance, assessments. Indicator scores are kept; audio is discarded.

CAPACITY

Each account carries a live 90-day forecast of delivery-ready seats against seats needed. Tenured attrition, new-hire attrition and seats in ramp are modelled separately, so an existing-business gap is seen as early as a ramp gap.

CAPABILITY

Each associate works one coaching topic for six weeks against a weekly step-up target the team leader can adjust, with the reason logged. As the simplest work is automated, the board shows who is ready for harder work.

SAFETY

The associate sees their own projection first and says what help they need. A monthly forum puts Operations, Training, Recruitment, HR, WFM, Pricing, Technology and Risk in one room, and treats every red flag as the organisation’s problem.

It is not an LMS, a training catalogue or another dashboard of lagging metrics. It does not rank associates, and it does not ask your client to pay more before it has proved anything. During the diagnostic, your extract is analysed by Treeng, SSUNDAR’s own sixteen-module analysis build — a delivery cost we absorb, not a product you buy.
