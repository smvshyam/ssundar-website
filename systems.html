SYSTEM 01

Judgment-Centered Leadership Design™.

Leaders are paid to make decisions under uncertainty. Not to demonstrate competencies on a matrix.

Traditional leadership development indexes on abstract buckets — "strategic thinking" and "executive presence" — that resist measurement and decay after the workshop high fades.

Judgment-Centered Design starts from a different premise. Every system component is architected around decision quality in specific contexts. Not personality. Not style. Judgment.
